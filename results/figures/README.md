# Iris Research Visualizations & Scientific Figures

This directory indexes the primary research figures and morphological anatomical diagrams used across the project documentation and publications.

---

## Figure Index

### 1. Specimen Morphology & Multi-Dimensional Distribution
- **Filename:** [`iris_specimen_morphology.png`](iris_specimen_morphology.png)
- **Format:** 24-bit PNG, $1920 \times 1080$ resolution.
- **Description:** Multi-panel visualization illustrating petal length vs. petal width bivariate distribution, sepal dimension clusters, kernel density estimations, and Gaussian Naive Bayes decision boundary contours across *I. setosa*, *I. versicolor*, and *I. virginica*.
- **Key Finding:** Confirms distinct petal-ratio clustering with $1.0$ separability for *setosa* and slight overlap at the versicolor/virginica morphological transition ($4.7\text{cm} - 5.0\text{cm}$ petal length).

### 2. Botanical Anatomy & Morphometric Landmarks
- **Filename:** [`iris_botany_anatomy.svg`](iris_botany_anatomy.svg)
- **Format:** Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG).
- **Description:** Vector anatomical diagram illustrating the standard botanical measurement landmarks of the Iridaceae flower, differentiating upright standard petals, downward falling sepals, perianth tubes, and dimensional calipers.
- **Scientific Reference:** Based on botanical taxonomical standards outlined in Anderson (1936) and Fisher (1936).
